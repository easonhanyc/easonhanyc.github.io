How I Work

Three things that show up in every case study above.

Non-goals are the product decision.
TripMatch shipped in six days because I wrote down what it would never do — no payments, no dispatch, no native app, no WhatsApp replacement — before writing any code. Scope discipline is the reason it exists at all.
I'd rather kill my own work than ship a known defect.
The easy path the day before TripMatch's launch was to ship. Instead I rebuilt the storage layer, because the version I'd already built would have silently deleted users' posts under exactly the load launch day produces.
I write the limitations down.
Every case study here ends with what I'd do differently, and the PRD carries its unresolved questions in the open. A portfolio that only contains wins isn't evidence of judgment — it's evidence of editing.
